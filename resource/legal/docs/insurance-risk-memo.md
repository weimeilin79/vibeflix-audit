RISK & COMPLIANCE MEMORANDUM
============================
Ref: RISK-2022-0047
Author: Sofia Andersson, Head of Risk
Effective: 2022-09-01
Classification: Internal

Subject: Product-Liability Insurance — Licensed Manufacturing

1. All licensed manufacturers must carry product-liability insurance of no less than
   **USD 5,000,000** per occurrence for the licensed product category, valid in the
   target territory.

2. Where a vendor's on-file coverage is insufficient for the category, Legal shall
   procure a category-specific **insurance rider** (reference format `RIDER-####`)
   PRIOR TO contract execution. Coverage is deemed granted upon rider issuance.

3. This USD 5,000,000 requirement **supersedes** the USD 2,000,000 figure quoted in the
   2019 onboarding SOP (SOP-LEG-014, §3). Please disregard the SOP figure.

4. Insurance verification is a hard gate. A contract must not be lodged (`LC-####`)
   while an insurance item is outstanding.

— S. Andersson
