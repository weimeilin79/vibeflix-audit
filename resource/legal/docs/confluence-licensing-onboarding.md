## Overview

When we onboard a manufacturing vendor for a **new product category**, Legal has to "do
the paperwork" before we can ship. This page is my attempt to write down what that
actually means, because honestly nobody fully agrees and it's never been in one place.

## The steps (as far as I can tell)

1. Draft a **license amendment** to the Master License Agreement adding the category.
   Register it → you get an `LA-####` reference. (There's a 2019 SOP for this but half of
   it is out of date, see `SOP-LEG-014`.)
2. **Certifications** — depends on the category. Ask Priya, she keeps the real list. I
   *think* plush needs EN71? See the cursed email thread.
3. **Customs / tariff code** — Logistics owns this. Kenji has a spreadsheet.
4. **Royalty rate** — Finance sets it. "12% standard" but there are volume tiers. Rate
   card is on SharePoint (link rotted, ask Ana).
5. **Insurance** — Risk team. $5M I'm fairly sure? There's a memo (RISK-2022-0047).
6. **Execute** the contract — counter-sign and it becomes an `LC-####` in the licensing
   registry.
