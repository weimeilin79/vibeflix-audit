# Licensing Royalty Rate Card

Owner: Ana Ruiz (Finance) · **Version 3** · 2023-02
⚠️ Version 2 is still floating around in email — ignore it, use this one.

**Base royalty:** 12% of wholesale price.

**Volume tiers** (annual units, per vendor — get the band from Vendor Clearance):

| Tier   | Annual units      | Royalty |
|--------|-------------------|---------|
| Tier 1 | < 50,000          | 12%     |
| Tier 2 | 50,000 – 250,000  | 10%     |
| Tier 3 | > 250,000         | 8%      |

**Category modifiers** (added on top of the tier rate):

| Category                        | Modifier |
|---------------------------------|----------|
| Apparel                         | +0%      |
| Plush                           | +0%      |
| Vinyl / Action Figures          | +0%      |
| Premium Collectibles / Resin    | **+2%**  |

> **IMPORTANT:** Legal must obtain the vendor's **current tier / annual-volume band from
> Vendor Clearance** before setting the rate. Do NOT default to 12% for a known
> high-volume vendor — Finance has been burned by this. The tier lives on the vendor
> record, not with us.
