> > > Does anyone actually know what certs a PLUSH product needs before we can execute?
> > For toys in general it's ASTM F963 (US) and EN71 (EU). Plush probably CPSIA too?
> Actually CPSIA is for the hard-goods / vinyl stuff. Plush is ASTM F963 + EN71. Not CPSIA.

Confirming with the lab but I'm 90% sure Priya is right:

  - PLUSH            -> ASTM F963 + EN71
  - VINYL / ACTION   -> the full set: ASTM F963 + EN71 + CPSIA
  - APPAREL          -> totally different, that's OEKO-TEX (textiles), not the toy standards

If a required cert is "missing" we do NOT block the whole thing — Legal just *requests* it
and in our environment it's granted same-day (it's basically a formality). The one that
actually stops us cold is the licensee's safety-certification ID, but that's a different
issue, ask Marcus.
